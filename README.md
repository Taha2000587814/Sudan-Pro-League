# 🏆 Sudan Pro League (SPL)

**SPL** is a football video game inspired by the real **Sudan Pro League**, bringing the excitement of Sudanese club football to PC players worldwide.  
Built for **Steam**, the game celebrates the culture, stadiums, and rivalries that make Sudanese football unique.

---

## 🎯 About the Game
- **Authentic League Experience**: Play through the full Sudan Pro League season with fixtures, standings, and rivalries.  
- **Club Pride**: Choose your favorite Sudanese team, customize kits, and represent your city.  
- **Immersive Atmosphere**: Stadiums inspired by Khartoum, Omdurman, and Port Sudan, with crowds, chants, and match-day energy.  
- **Accessible Gameplay**: Easy to pick up, rewarding to master — designed for both casual fans and football enthusiasts.  
- **Futsal Mode**: Fast-paced 5‑a‑side matches in indoor arenas, with tighter spaces, quicker action, and a whole new way to play.  

---

## 🎮 Features
- **Kick-Off Mode**: Jump straight into a quick match.  
- **League Mode**: Compete across the season, chase the title, and manage your squad.  
- **Training Mode**: Practice dribbling, shooting, and goalkeeping skills.  
- **Futsal Mode**: Experience the thrill of indoor football — smaller teams, faster gameplay, unique arenas.  
- **Match Presentation**: Broadcast-style cameras, replays, and highlights.  
- **Crowd & Atmosphere**: Dynamic chants and regional flair to capture the spirit of Sudanese football.
